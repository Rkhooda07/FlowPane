# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 24, 2026**

FlowPane is a desktop utility designed with privacy in mind.

## 1. Data Collection
FlowPane **does not collect any personal data**. All information you enter or tasks you create are stored locally on your device.

## 2. Data Transmission
No data leaves your device. FlowPane does not have a backend server and does not transmit your data to any third party.

## 3. Analytics and Crash Reports
FlowPane does not use any third-party analytics or crash reporting services that collect identifying information.

## 4. Local Storage
FlowPane uses local storage (SQLite or JSON files) to persist your settings and tasks. This data remains on your machine.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub Issues on our repository.
