Behaviour

Four states, one gesture.

Drag the pane at a screen edge and it works through the whole sequence on its own. Nothing to configure, nothing to learn.

  1. 01

    Float

    Sits above every window, in every space. Your list never falls behind a browser tab.

  2. 02

    Collapse

    Push it to any screen edge and it folds straight to a sliver — one motion, no separate lock step. Just the eyes and a running clock.

  3. 03

    Peek

    Bring the cursor close and it unfolds. Move away and it tucks back. No clicking.

  4. 04

    Restore

    Grab the sliver and pull it away from the edge — it springs back to full size, right where your cursor is.

macOS says "unidentified developer". Is that a problem?

No. It means the app isn't signed with a $99/yr Apple Developer ID — a certificate that proves who built something, not what it does. Right-click the app and choose Open, or allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security. The source is public if you'd rather check it yourself.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Tasks, notes and settings live in a single tasks.json on your Mac. FlowPane makes zero network requests — no analytics, no crash reports, no update checks. Even the typeface is bundled rather than fetched.

Intel Macs? Windows?

Not yet, on both counts. The current build is Apple Silicon only. A Windows build is in progress — Tauri handles most of it, but the edge-snap and transparency work is platform-specific. Watch the repo to hear about either.
