Built for flow

Every detail designed to keep you in the zone — not managing your tools.

Floating, always-on-top pane

FlowPane lives quietly above every other window. Your tasks, notes, and timer stay visible while you work — no context switching, no alt-tabbing, no losing your place.

Edge snapping & collapse

Drag to any screen edge to snap. Pull further to collapse into a minimal sliver — just your active task and a timer, always glanceable, never in the way.

Hover to peek

No click required. Move your cursor near a collapsed edge and FlowPane reveals itself instantly. Pull away and it tucks back — fluid, frictionless, intentional.

Quick task capture

Type naturally — "/today 3pm", "!urgent", "@2h" — and FlowPane parses dates, urgency, and timers on the fly. Capture thoughts at the speed of thinking.

Focus Mode

Enter a dedicated full-screen view with a countdown or stopwatch. Everything else dims. Just you, your task, and the clock. Finish with a moment of celebration, not a notification.

Multi-note workspace

Color-coded note tabs let you keep parallel threads — specs, ideas, references — organized within a single task. Switch contexts with a click, not a window hunt.

The Eye Bubble

A small, friendly nudge when you've drifted. No nagging sounds, no red badges — just a quiet "hey, still working on this?" that respects your agency.

Multi-window support

Spawn independent FlowPane instances for different contexts — work, personal, a specific project. Each remembers its position, tasks, and collapsed state.

Questions?

Is this app safe?

Yes. FlowPane is open source (MIT licensed) — you can audit every line of code on GitHub.

The "unidentified developer" prompt appears because I haven't paid for an Apple Developer ID ($99/yr). That certificate only verifies who built the app, not what it does. No telemetry, no network calls, no hidden behavior. Your trust is earned by transparency, not a certificate.

Does this work on Intel Macs?

Not yet. The current build is Apple Silicon only (M1/M2/M3/M4). An Intel (x86_64) build is planned — it requires testing on Intel hardware I don't currently have access to.

If you're on Intel and want to be notified, open a GitHub issue or star the repo to track progress.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All tasks, notes, and settings live in a local tasks.json file on your Mac. FlowPane makes zero network requests — no analytics, no crash reporting, no update checks that phone home. Even the font (Outfit) is self-hosted and bundled with the app.

Your data never leaves your device. Period.

Is Windows supported?

Not yet. A Windows build is in active development — Tauri makes it straightforward, but the window management APIs (edge-snap, always-on-top, transparency) need platform-specific work.

Want a heads-up when it's ready? Follow the repo or join discussions.
